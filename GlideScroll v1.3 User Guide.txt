==================================================
          GlideScroll v1.3  User Guide
==================================================

In one sentence: GlideScroll makes your mouse wheel scroll
smoothly instead of jumping line by line, and it turns your
middle mouse button into a click-and-drag pan, like sliding a
sheet of paper around the screen.

You do not need to understand anything technical. Install it,
use your computer normally, and adjust things only if you feel
like it. The defaults are tuned to feel good out of the gate.


--------------------------------------------------
 GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------

1. Find the GlideScroll icon in your system tray (bottom-right of
   the screen, near the clock). You may need to click the small
   "^" arrow to see it.

2. Double-click the icon to open the Settings window. Right-click
   it for a quick menu, including Exit to fully quit.

3. GlideScroll runs quietly in the background and starts with
   Windows. Set it and forget it.

The Settings window has three tabs: Scrolling, Panning, and
App rules.


--------------------------------------------------
 GAME MODE   (App rules tab)
--------------------------------------------------

On the App rules tab there is a button with a game controller
icon: Game Mode. Click it to open the Game Mode window.

What it does, in plain words: when Game Mode is on and you are in
a game, GlideScroll pauses itself. The moment you are back on a
normal window (your browser, Discord, a document), smooth
scrolling picks right back up. You never have to touch anything.

How it knows you are in a game: it recognizes well-known games by
name (there is a list inside the Game Mode window, already filled
in), it recognizes anti-cheat systems when they are running, and
it recognizes fullscreen games in general. You can add any game it
misses with "Browse for .exe" or by dragging "Point at a window"
onto the running game.

While you are in a game, scrolling feels exactly as if GlideScroll
were not installed: weapon switching on the wheel stays instant
and crisp, and GlideScroll stays completely clear of anti-cheat
systems. With two monitors, a browser on the second screen stays
smooth while the game runs on the first. Hover the tray icon while
paused and the tooltip names the reason.

Game Mode is on by default. If you never play games, you will
simply never notice it.


--------------------------------------------------
 SCROLLING FEELS   (Scrolling tab)
--------------------------------------------------

The "Smoothing engine" dropdown changes how scrolling feels. The
list is ordered roughly from most smoothed to least smoothed:

  * LIQUID GLIDE - the smoothest, glides with inertia.
  * SILK - the default. As smooth as Liquid Glide but it
    settles quickly, no drifting after you stop.
  * CADENCE - adapts to you: crisp when reading slowly, momentum
    when you flick fast.
  * SMOOTHED NOTCH - stock-style steps, but softened.
  * NOTCH WITH GLIDE - stock-style steps with a mild glide.
  * LOW LATENCY - minimal smoothing, fastest response.

Try a few in your daily apps and pick what feels right; every
engine responds to the sliders below it. Scroll one way then
quickly flick the other: it stops cleanly instead of bouncing.

The "Optimize for monitor" button reads your screen's refresh
rate and resolution and tunes glide duration and step size to
match. Worth one click on a new install.


--------------------------------------------------
 PANNING   (Panning tab)
--------------------------------------------------

Hold the MIDDLE mouse button (press the wheel straight down) and
drag to pan around, like a touchpad.

Two pan engines, picked from the dropdown at the top:

  * FEATHER (default) - super light and immediate. The page sticks
    to your hand and stops the instant you stop. Paper under your
    palm.
  * CUSHION - the softer, damped feel with a smoothing filter.
    Heavier, more of a glide.

With Flick mode on, release mid-drag and the page coasts to a
stop; Coast Friction sets how quickly. A plain middle-click still
works normally everywhere (opening links in new tabs, closing
browser tabs), GlideScroll only takes over when you hold and drag.


--------------------------------------------------
 THEMES
--------------------------------------------------

Open the gear icon (top right of the settings window) to pick a
look: Auto, Light, Dark, OLED Electric, Cozy Pink, Cozy Blue,
Sakura Pop, Lavender Milk, Grid Graphite, Aizome Indigo, Neon
Dusk, Cyan Circuit, Green Phosphor, Amber CRT, Abyss Glow, plus
three Custom slots of your own. Auto follows your Windows
light/dark setting.

  - EVERY theme is editable. The six color boxes at the bottom of
    Preferences always show the selected theme's colors; change
    any of them and it saves by itself.
  - The Reset button next to the theme dropdown puts ONLY the
    currently selected theme back to its factory colors.
  - Custom 1, 2, and 3 are three separate palettes you can build
    from scratch. The color boxes accept hex (#FFFFFF or FFFFFF),
    rgb(255,255,255), or a click on the swatch to open a picker.


--------------------------------------------------
 APP RULES   (App rules tab)
--------------------------------------------------

Two modes, and both lists are saved separately so switching modes
never loses your work:

  * EXCLUDE THESE APPS - GlideScroll runs everywhere except the
    apps you list. This is the default, pre-loaded with the two
    places Windows handles scrolling oddly (File Explorer and
    Photo Viewer).
  * ONLY RUN FOR THESE APPS - GlideScroll stays off everywhere
    except the apps you list.

Add apps with "Add running app", "Browse for .exe", or point at a
window. You can also right-click the tray icon to instantly
disable GlideScroll for whatever app is in the foreground.


--------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING FEELS OFF
--------------------------------------------------

  - Scrolling feels wrong in one specific app: add that app on the
    App rules tab and it goes back to stock behavior there.
  - Want everything back to stock for a moment: right-click the
    tray icon and pause or Exit. GlideScroll changes nothing
    permanent on your system.
  - A setting seems stuck: settings live in one file at
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\GlideScroll\config.json. Deleting it returns
    every setting to factory defaults on the next start.

Found a bug or have an idea? Reports are very welcome on the
project's GitHub page. The most helpful report is: which app you
were in, what you did, what happened, and what you expected
instead. "This felt weird" with an app name is plenty.

Enjoy the glide.
